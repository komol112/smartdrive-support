🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Your privacy is highly important to us. This section explains how the Smart Drive mobile application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Location Data: Smart Drive requires access to your device's location services to track mileage and provide core background car services. This data is processed in real-time to ensure features function correctly even when the app is closed or not in use.

Personal Data: We secure your account via phone number authentication. No personal information or location data is shared with or sold to third parties.

2. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, encryption, and secure cloud servers to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

3. Consent

By using our app, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms regarding location tracking for mileage logs.
